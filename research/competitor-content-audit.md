## SITE PROFILES

### 1. peptidesupermarket.co.uk/research
- **Nature:** Predominantly a price comparison engine (96 peptides, 265+ UK suppliers). Not a research content site.
- **Research section:** The /research path timed out (504). Research and Guides section listed in sitemap but article paths return 404.
- **Article titles found via sitemap crawl:**
  - BPC-157 Playbook: The Master Key to Repair
  - Cagrilintide Explained: The Amylin Signal Behind Fullness
  - CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin: The Truth About the Recovery and Fat Loss Stack
  - GLP-1s Aren't Miracle Drugs or Metabolism Killers
  - MOTS-C: the mitochondrial signal your body is missing
  - Retatrutide: why microdosing is wrong
  - Semaglutide and Tirzepatide: Why You Have Been Played
  - The Wolverine Protocol
  - Why Glutathione Delivery Matters More Than Most People Think
- **Tone:** Strong editorial voice, contrarian angles, challenges common advice.
- **Key limitation:** Actual article content not accessible. Site is price comparison engine with thin editorial overlay.

### 2. pepguide.io
- **Nature:** Full peptide encyclopedia - 700+ peptides, 8 major categories, dedicated research articles.
- **Structure:** Category pages into individual profiles into research articles, comparisons, stacks.
- **Content quality:** Detailed mechanisms, dosing protocols, reconstitution calculators, molecular properties. Sources cited.
- **Research articles:** Complete guides (BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu, Epithalon), comparisons, stack guides, practical guides.

### 3. peptideguide.com
- **Nature:** Structured evidence-led reference - 58 profiles, 62 comparisons, 17 goal pages, 26 mechanisms.
- **Structure:** Peptide profiles into Goal pages into Comparison pages into Research Hub. Evidence-graded.
- **Content quality:** Clean, structured, uses rating system (1-10) per goal area. Evidence quality labels (Moderate/Limited/Preclinical). Updated April 2026.
- **Unique features:** Snapshot ratings per peptide across goal areas, comparison tables, evidence-level metadata.

## TOPIC 4: GHK-Cu for Skin/Hair

### peptidesupermarket.co.uk
- Price description: Promotes collagen synthesis, reduces fine lines and wrinkles
- Price range: from 6.48 to 222.01 across 304 suppliers
- No dedicated article accessible

### pepguide.io
- **Profile:** GHK-Cu Research Profile, Mechanism and Protocols
- **Key data:** Naturally occurring copper-binding tripeptide; blood levels decline from 200 ng/mL (age 20) to 80 ng/mL (age 60)
- **Gene impact:** Alters 32.1% of human genes; upregulates regenerative genes, downregulates inflammatory genes
- **vs minoxidil:** 1 microM GHK-Cu comparable to 5% minoxidil for DP cell proliferation
- **Safety:** Topical up to 3% with no contact dermatitis concerns
- **Mechanisms:** Copper-mediated redox, NFkB suppression, DNA repair, proteasome cleaning
- **Dosing:** Injectable 1-2 mg, topical 0.5-1%, once daily
- **Unique angle:** Microneedling + GHK-Cu protocol for hair restoration

### peptideguide.com
- **Profile:** GHK-Cu Peptide Guide updated April 11, 2026
- **Evidence grading:** Moderate for topical skin, Limited for injectable tissue repair, Preclinical for neuroprotection/longevity
- **Key data:** Gene array study showing influence over 30% of age-dysregulated genes
- **Clinical signals:** Measurable skin improvement over 8-12 weeks topical; faster epithelialisation in animals
- **Mechanisms:** Copper delivery, TGF-beta pathway, MMP regulation, VEGF/FGF upregulation
- **Key quote:** Topical evidence base meaningfully stronger than injectable
