# TrustScore Ranking System

The TrustScore ranking for UK peptide suppliers, out of 100.

**Automated (we check their site) — up to 55:**
- COA / lab results    +25
- Customer reviews      +10
- Live contact          +10
- Tracked shipping       +5
- Research-use compliance +5

**Free badge (optional) — +20:**
- Supplier adds our TrustScore badge code to their site (links back to ViralPeps)

**Business audit (£50/month) — +10 to +25:**
- Card/bank + verified business  → +25
- Crypto + Ltd company           → +20
- Crypto + sole trader           → +10

**Max = 100.**

Rules:
- Real-money payments (card/bank) outrank crypto.
- A business that takes card/bank reaches the top band.
- Scores are never for sale — the audit fee simply covers us re-confirming the
  business every month.
