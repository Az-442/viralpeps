NAD⁺ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) – Research Compound

At Aegis Peptides, NAD⁺ is supplied as a high-purity, research-grade coenzyme in lyophilised form, designed for stability, accuracy, and reproducibility in scientific applications. NAD⁺ is a fundamental molecule widely studied in cellular metabolism, mitochondrial function, and ageing-related research. It plays a key role in redox reactions and cellular energy production, making it a central focus in advanced metabolic and biochemical studies.

Handling, Storage & Usage
NAD⁺ is provided as a stable lyophilised compound for accurate reconstitution in controlled laboratory environments. To maintain integrity and stability, store in accordance with recommended conditions and avoid repeated freeze–thaw cycles. Standard sterile laboratory procedures should be followed during handling and preparation where applicable.

Prior to reconstitution, allow both the compound and diluent to reach room temperature to minimise thermal stress. Use aseptic techniques and avoid excessive dilution to help preserve compound stability in solution.
