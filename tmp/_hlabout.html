Healius Peptides is a research peptide company supplying high-purity research peptides to laboratories, contract research organizations, and independent researchers across the United States, the United Kingdom, the EU, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand. The catalog covers more than 40 pure peptides at 99%+ HPLC purity, every batch independently third-party tested, and every order shipped with a full Certificate of Analysis. Eleven years of consistent quality, more than 35,000 customers served, and a 4.7-star average across thousands of verified researcher reviews. The peptide company researchers trust to deliver on the data, not on the marketing.

Why choose Healius Peptides?

Reliable Quality

Every Healius peptide is independently third-party tested at an ISO 17025 accredited laboratory and verified to exceed 99% HPLC purity. Identity is confirmed by mass spectrometry on every batch. The full analytical panel covers purity, identity, endotoxin, heavy metals, microbial bioburden, residual solvents, and water content. Every result is published on the Certificate of Analysis attached to your order.

Fast Service

Same-business-day dispatch on every order placed before the daily cutoff. Free express shipping worldwide via Royal Mail (UK) and DHL Express (international). Tracked end-to-end with typical delivery in 1 business day domestic UK, 2 to 5 business days priority international, and 3 to 7 business days standard international.

Temperature-Controlled Storage

Healius lyophilized peptides are held at minus 20 degrees Celsius from synthesis through to dispatch. Inventory is stored in laboratory-grade freezers and is handled only briefly in the cold chain during picking. Temperature exposure is one of the quietest causes of peptide degradation between the supplier and the lab; we engineered it out of our logistics.

Experienced Support

Real humans, no AI, no overseas call centers. People with backgrounds in the peptide industry and analytical chemistry staff support. Text us for a reply in minutes during business hours, or email for longer technical inquiries with a typical reply window of one hour.

Fair Pricing

Healius prices reflect the cost of synthesis at qualified contract manufacturers, plus the cost of independent third-party testing, cold chain logistics, and a sustainable margin. We do not run permanent fake discounts. Bulk and research partner accounts unlock quarterly-reviewed tier pricing, and our Bundle and Save catalog offers genuine multi-vial discounts.

Strict Compliance

All Healius products are supplied for in vitro research use only and not for human or veterinary administration. Research-use-only labeling is on every vial, every shipment, and every Certificate of Analysis. Buyers are responsible for ensuring compliance with their jurisdiction’s laws, and we publish full compliance documentation on the terms and conditions page.

Our commitment to research

Healius supplies research peptides only. Every product in the catalog is for in vitro laboratory and preclinical use, not for human or veterinary administration, and we do not provide dosing, clinical, or medical guidance under any circumstances. Our quality assurance process exists for one reason: to make sure the peptide in your vial is exactly what the label says it is, with the analytical data attached to prove it. Every batch ships with a Certificate of Analysis that documents identity, purity, endotoxin level, heavy metals, microbial bioburden, residual solvents, and water content. The data travels with the product, so the researcher can verify before reconstituting.

Manufacturing partnership

Healius commissions peptide synthesis from a panel of accredited contract manufacturing organizations operating under documented quality management systems. Each manufacturing partner is qualified against a minimum specification covering sequence fidelity, residual solvent management, counterion control, and cold-chain handling from the synthesis bench to the dispatch warehouse. Direct relationships with our manufacturing partners allow us to audit synthesis records, request additional analytical work when the research protocol calls for it, and reject any batch that does not meet our specifications before it reaches our inventory.

What matters to us

  • We ship what was ordered from the lot documented on the Certificate of Analysis: no substitutions, no unlabeled vials, no compromises on identity.
  • We publish the analytical data behind every purity claim. Chromatograms, mass spectrometry traces, and third-party verification certificates are all on the COA, not buried in marketing language.
  • We maintain full research-use-only compliance at all times to protect the researchers who use our peptides and the integrity of the data those peptides support.
  • We provide the same care and service to a single-vial order from an independent researcher as to a 1,000-vial standing order from a global contract research organization.

What sets Healius apart from other peptide companies

The research peptide market has matured significantly over the last five years. Researchers compare suppliers on five things: purity standard, third-party verification, batch traceability, payment security and customer support quality. Healius leads on all five. The 99%+ HPLC purity standard is hard, not soft. Every batch is independently verified by an ISO 17025 accredited third-party laboratory. Every vial links to a unique batch record traceable from synthesis to your bench. Every order is paid through major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), giving the buyer card-network consumer protection most peptide companies do not provide. Every customer enquiry is handled by a real human in business hours, not a bot.

Three numbers tell the story. 35,000 customers across 11 years of trading. 4.7-star average across thousands of verified researcher reviews. 390,000+ orders shipped without a single regulatory action. Compare those figures against any other research peptide company before you place your first order.

If you are looking for a research peptide company that publishes its analytical data, ships fast worldwide, and accepts secure card payment, browse the full Healius catalog. Every product comes with a batch-specific Certificate of Analysis, a 60-day money-back guarantee on purity, and same-business-day dispatch on orders placed before the cutoff.
