Research FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this product suitable for human or veterinary use?

No. All NovaVitality compounds are supplied strictly for in-vitro laboratory research by qualified researchers and institutions. They are not medicines, food, supplements or cosmetics and must not be administered to humans or animals.

How should the lyophilised powder be stored?

Store unopened vials at or below −20 °C, protected from light and moisture. Once reconstituted, divide into aliquots and avoid repeated freeze–thaw cycles, which are a common cause of lost purity. Product-specific notes are given in the details above.

Where can I find the Certificate of Analysis?

Where we hold a certificate for the current batch, you can download it from the Certificate of Analysis panel beside the product image and match the batch number on your vial label against it. If there’s no panel, email us with your batch number and we’ll send the documentation we hold. Our COA Library lists every published certificate and explains how to read one.

What solvent is typically used for reconstitution?

Laboratory protocols commonly use bacteriostatic water or another research-grade solvent appropriate to the assay. Always follow your institution’s standard operating procedures.
