Shipping & Delivery

Every order leaves our UK facility cold-packed, tamper-sealed and tracked. Here is exactly what to expect.

Dispatch

  • Orders paid before 2:00 pm GMT on a working day are dispatched the same day; later payments dispatch the next working day.
  • Products are picked from temperature-monitored storage and packed in thermal-insulated packaging with a tamper-evident seal.
  • The outer box is plain.

Tracking

Your tracking number is emailed the moment your order ships. If tracking has not updated for 3 working days (UK) or 10 working days (international), contact [email protected] and we will investigate with the courier.

International orders

International buyers are responsible for ensuring the products can be lawfully imported into their country, and for any customs duties or taxes. Research documentation can be included with the shipment on request.

On arrival

Inspect the parcel on delivery. If the tamper seal is broken or contents are damaged, photograph everything and report it to us within 48 hours — this is the only window in which a damaged, faulty or incorrect item can be resolved; see our Returns & Refunds Policy. Refrigerate pen products (2–8 °C) promptly; see Storage & Handling.

About Peptide Global

Peptide Global is a United Kingdom–based supplier of premium research-grade peptides serving academic institutions, contract research organisations and independent laboratories in more than thirty countries. Our catalogue focuses on pre-mixed, stabilised formats that are ready for the bench, prepared under controlled conditions and handled with the care that sensitive research compounds demand, from the moment they arrive at our facility to the moment they leave it.

Our fulfilment operation maintains careful temperature control from goods-in to dispatch. Products are held in monitored storage, picked to order, and packed in thermal-insulated, tamper-evident packaging. Orders placed before 2:00 pm GMT are dispatched the same working day with tracked delivery across the UK, the European Union and worldwide, and our technical team supports researchers with storage, handling and delivery questions within one working day.

All products sold by Peptide Global are supplied strictly for laboratory research use only. They are not for human or veterinary use, and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Product descriptions and associated literature are provided for informational purposes only and do not constitute medical advice.
